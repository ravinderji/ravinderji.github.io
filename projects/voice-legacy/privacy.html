Voice Legacy: Stories & Voices (Android) · Effective 24 October 2026

In short: what you do in the app stays on your phone. We don't have accounts, servers, ads or tracking, and we never receive your stories, recordings or browsing.

Who we are

Voice Legacy is made by an independent developer in India ("we"). Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are responsible for the little personal data we receive, described below. Contact: support@codetumblr.net.

What is kept, and where

  • Web pages you have read aloud are not stored unless you choose to save them.
  • Stories, the books and pages you save, your playlists and your list of websites are stored only on your phone, each encrypted with its own key held in the phone's secure key store (Android Keystore).
  • Voice recordings and voice profiles of your loved ones are stored the same way.
  • When you delete something, its key is destroyed and the file overwritten, so it can't be recovered.
  • The app's data is excluded from cloud backups and from transfers to a new phone.
  • Backups: a backup is a file you make and save where you choose, encrypted with a passphrase only you know. We never receive it, and nobody can open it without the passphrase.
  • PDF and ePub books you import are read on the phone; the file itself isn't sent anywhere.

When the app uses the internet

Only when you ask it to:

  • Opening a link or browsing a website: the website receives your request like in any browser (your IP address, and its own cookies). That website's own privacy policy applies to it. The app's browser loads text only (no pictures, video or audio), blocks known ad and tracking networks and cross-site cookies, never lets a site use the camera, microphone or location, and asks before a link takes you to another website. It keeps a site's own cookies and saved data so sites keep working; you can erase them with "Clear website data".
  • Searching for or downloading free books: Wikisource receives your search words and your IP address.
  • Nothing else is sent: reading aloud, voice copying and everything else run on the phone itself.

Emails you send us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply to you, never for marketing, and delete it within 12 months of your last message.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you record a voice.
  • Camera or photos: only when you scan a page. The photo is read on the phone and erased afterwards. The scanner uses Google ML Kit on the phone; Google may receive anonymous usage and performance statistics from it.
  • You can turn these permissions off at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Voice Legacy › Permissions; the rest of the app keeps working.

Children

Voice Legacy is meant for people aged 13 and over. Anyone under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission. The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children; it has nowhere to send it.

Your rights

Under Indian law you can ask to see, correct or erase personal data about you, name someone to act for you if you can't, and have a complaint answered.

  • Everything on your phone is under your control: view, edit or delete it in the app, or erase it all with Settings › About › Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.
  • For emails you have sent us, write to support@codetumblr.net. We reply within 30 days.
  • If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If something goes wrong

Your stories and recordings never leave your phone, so a breach on our side can't expose them. If emails you sent us were ever exposed, we would tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the app will show the new version. Questions: support@codetumblr.net.

  • जिन वेब पेजों को आपने पढ़कर सुना, वे तब तक नहीं रखे जाते जब तक आप उन्हें सहेजें नहीं।

  • अपनों की आवाज़ की रिकॉर्डिंग और आवाज़ की प्रोफ़ाइल भी इसी तरह रखी जाती हैं।
  • जब आप कुछ मिटाते हैं, उसकी कुंजी नष्ट कर दी जाती है और फ़ाइल को ओवरराइट किया जाता है, इसलिए उसे वापस नहीं लाया जा सकता।
  • ऐप का डेटा क्लाउड बैकअप और नए फ़ोन में ट्रांसफ़र से बाहर रखा गया है।
  • बैकअप: बैकअप एक फ़ाइल है जिसे आप बनाते हैं और अपनी चुनी जगह सहेजते हैं, सिर्फ़ आपको पता पासफ़्रेज़ से एन्क्रिप्टेड। वह कभी हम तक नहीं पहुँचती, और पासफ़्रेज़ के बिना कोई उसे नहीं खोल सकता।
  • आप जो PDF और ePub किताबें जोड़ते हैं, वे फ़ोन पर ही पढ़ी जाती हैं; फ़ाइल कहीं नहीं भेजी जाती।

  • मुफ़्त किताबें खोजना या डाउनलोड करना: विकिस्रोत को आपके खोज-शब्द और IP पता मिलता है।
  • और कुछ नहीं भेजा जाता: पढ़कर सुनाना, आवाज़ की कॉपी और बाक़ी सब फ़ोन पर ही होता है।
