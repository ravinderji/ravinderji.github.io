Stories, books and letters, read aloud in voices you love.

Voice Legacy reads anything aloud in natural Hindi and English voices, and can read in a voice like a loved one's. Everything happens on your phone: no account, no ads, no cloud.

✨ Features

  • 📖 Hear anything read aloud: type or paste text, open a file, scan a printed page, share from any app, or read the story on a web page.
  • 🗣️ 16 natural voices: Hindi, Indian, US and UK English, male and female, with moods from warm to bedtime calm, and a voice for each character in a dialogue.
  • ❤️ A loved one's voice: record a family member (with their permission) and hear stories in a voice like theirs. Kept private and encrypted on your phone.
  • 🪔 580+ built-in stories: Panchatantra, Akbar–Birbal, Jataka, Aesop, folk and fairy tales and poems, in Hindi and English.
  • 📚 Free classic books: Premchand, Kabir, Tagore, Vivekananda and more from Wikisource, shown only where their copyright has ended in your country; your own PDF and ePub books too.
  • 🌙 Made for long listening: reads with the screen off, lock-screen and headphone controls, sleep timer, continue where you stopped.
  • 🌐 English and Hindi app: light, dark, sepia and night themes, adjustable text size.

🔒 Privacy

Speech is made on your phone, and your stories, books and voice recordings never leave it; they are encrypted, and what you delete can't be recovered. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use (also in हिन्दी).
