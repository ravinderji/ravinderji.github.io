Voice Legacy: Stories & Voices

कहानियाँ सुनें, अपनों की आवाज़ में

Stories, books and letters, read aloud in natural Hindi and English voices, or in a voice like a loved one's. Everything happens on your phone: no account, no ads, no cloud.

Coming soon on Google Play

What it does

  • 📖
    Hear anything read aloudType or paste text, open a file, scan a printed page, share from any app, or read the story on a web page.
  • 🗣️
    16 natural voicesHindi, Indian, US and UK English, male and female, with moods from warm to bedtime calm, and a voice for each character in a dialogue.
  • ❤️
    A loved one's voiceRecord a family member (with their permission) and hear stories in a voice like theirs. Kept private and encrypted on your phone.
  • 🪔
    580+ built-in storiesPanchatantra, Akbar–Birbal, Jataka, Aesop, folk and fairy tales and poems, in Hindi and English.
  • 📚
    Free classic booksPremchand, Kabir, Tagore, Vivekananda and more from Wikisource, shown only where their copyright has ended in your country; your own PDF and ePub books too.
  • 🌙
    Made for long listeningReads with the screen off, lock-screen and headphone controls, sleep timer, continue where you stopped.
  • 🌐
    English and Hindi appLight, dark, sepia and night themes, adjustable text size.

Your privacy

Speech is made on your phone, and your stories, books and voice recordings never leave it. They are encrypted, and what you delete can't be recovered.
