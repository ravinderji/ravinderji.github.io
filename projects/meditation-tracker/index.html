Meditation Tracker: Japa Mala

मंत्र जप काउंटर

Chant aloud and every jaap is counted. A digital mala that listens while you chant and moves one bead for every repetition. Hands free, eyes closed if you like.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • 🎙️
    Counts by listeningOffline speech recognition on your phone, even with the screen off.
  • 📿
    Over 180 traditional mantrasWith purpose and meaning in English and Hindi, in Devanagari or Roman letters.
  • 🎯
    Targets and malas27, 54, 108 or your own number; Rudraksha, Tulsi, Sandalwood, Sphatik or Haldi mala.
  • 🙏
    Sankalpa (anushthan)For example 11 malas a day for 40 days, tracked day by day.
  • ⏰
    Chant remindersDaily times or a wake-up alarm, with a streak of days in a row.
  • 🌙
    PanchangToday's tithi, sunrise and sunset, Ekadashi and festivals ahead, each with a mantra.
  • 🌐
    English and HindiLight and dark themes. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Your privacy

Your chanting is counted on your phone and never sent anywhere. Your mantras and counts stay on your phone.
